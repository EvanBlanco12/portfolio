# A9 – [Pulley System]

## Objective
The purpose of this project was to design a belt and pulley assembly that could transmit rotational motion between two separate shafts. The design allows for a shaft spacing of 4 to 6 inches, with each pulley measuring 3 inches across. Operating conditions include a rotational speed between 1000 and 2000 RPM and an initial belt tension ranging from 4 to 6 lbf. Materials such as steel, aluminum, and ABS plastic are acceptable for manufacturing the pulleys. The shaft openings and belt grooves can be modified based on the design, provided the system maintains a safety factor of 3.

## Lesson Learned
Throughout this assignment, I gained a better understanding of how different parts of a mechanical design work together and how important it is to consider multiple factors when making design decisions. I learned that changing something as simple as belt tension or the distance between pulleys can affect the overall performance of the system. Another important lesson was understanding how material selection and the availability of certain parts can influence a design. Using Ashby plots also helped me compare materials and determine which options would work best for the project.

I also realized that many of the calculations and concepts used in this assignment were similar to things I had previously learned in statics and solid mechanics. This showed me how important it is to understand earlier material because engineering concepts continue to build on one another. At first, the assignment seemed like it would be difficult, but breaking it into smaller sections made it much easier to complete. Overall, this project helped improve my understanding of mechanical design, problem-solving, and material selection.

This assignment took me approximately 3 hours to complete.
