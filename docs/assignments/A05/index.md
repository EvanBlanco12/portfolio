## Objective
The objective of this project is to design a symmetric bracket for supporting a symmetric load of 500–800 lbf from the rigid T-beam shown in Figure 1. The bracket will have a safety factor of 4 and will be designed using Aluminum 6061-T6, ASTM A36 Steel, or Ti-6Al-4V Titanium. The load will be carried using Uline Heavy Duty Polyester Cord Strapping (3/4" × 2,500'). Stress and stiffness analyses will be completed for the required fit classes ("a," "b," and "c"), with an allowable deflection of no more than 0.005 inches. The dimensions obtained from the stress and stiffness calculations will be compared to determine which condition controls the design. The completed bracket will then be verified through free-body diagrams and calculations, followed by the creation of three-view engineering drawings.
