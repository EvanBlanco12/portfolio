What does it mean to defend an Engineering Decision?
To defend an Engineering Decision is to be able to explain the reasoning behind why you choose a certain design and solution. When you're explaining your design or solution this should mainly be facts. As an engineer it's your responsibility to show your proof this can either be graphics, pictures, calculations, or testing. I don't believe I fully understand how to defend an engineering decision yet. Throughout this semester I believe I will learn how to fully demonstrate what this looks like and what it exactly means.
